# The Tree of Life — origin and founding lineages

*Planet `06cy8w6z6a89kow6psje93` · the deep biological canon on which every
regional flora, fauna, and people is built.*

This is the **root** of the life layer: how life began on this world, the
chemistry it runs on, the handful of ancestral stocks everything descends
from, and when the great branchings happened — pegged to the dated
geological and climate record in [`../GEOLOGICAL_HISTORY.md`](../GEOLOGICAL_HISTORY.md)
and [`../PALEOCLIMATE.md`](../PALEOCLIMATE.md). Later documents (biogeographic
realms, then regional ecologies and peoples) hang off this one and must not
contradict it.

### How to read the labels

Following the Phase-5 convention (see the worldbuilding skill):

- **MEASURED** — a physical fact taken straight from the generated dataset.
- **INTERPRETED** — my reading of the physical record (e.g. mapping the
  lettered cratons of the geological history onto the four continents).
- **INVENTED** — a free creative choice, consistent with the data but not
  forced by it. All biology below the level of "there could be life here" is
  ultimately invented; the point is that it is invented *from* the physics,
  not against it.

Coined names (pigments, domains, clades) are **provisional working labels**,
exactly like the continent names Meridia/Sirocca/Selvana/Borea — placeholders
to be replaced when the cultures who would name them are written.

## 1. Why life began in the sea, at the vents

**MEASURED.** This is an ocean world: **79.1 % of the surface is sea**, the
ocean reaches **−9.28 km** at its deepest, and the crust is tectonically busy
(80 plates, a continuous girdle of active margins, five mapped Large Igneous
Provinces in the last 750 Myr). Three facts follow that matter for genesis:

1. **Abundant liquid water** across a temperate range (global mean ≈ 17 °C;
   liquid ocean from the tropics to the ice margins). Water is the solvent.
2. **Deep, permanent, high-pressure hadal trenches** — a refuge that never
   freezes and never boils, buffered from everything happening at the surface.
3. **Vigorous hydrothermal circulation** at spreading ridges and back-arcs,
   pumping mineral-laden, chemically-disequilibrium fluid into cold seawater.

**INVENTED (from the physics).** Life originated at the **alkaline
hydrothermal vents** of the mid-ocean ridges, where the mineral gradient
between reduced vent fluid and oxidized seawater supplied the first free
energy — chemistry before sunlight. The deep ocean and hadal trenches then
served as the **cradle and the vault**: every later planetary catastrophe
(above all the S1 icehouse, §5) was survivable because there was always a
deep, dark, warm-enough refuge for the ancestral stock to wait it out. This
world's life is *marine to its core*; land life is a late and repeated
invasion, never the main event (§6, §7).

## 2. Core biochemistry

**INVENTED, constrained by the temperate-water chemistry above.** The
sensible, parsimonious baseline — carbon backbone, water solvent — because the
climate the generator produced is Earth-like enough that exotic solvents
(ammonia, methane) are neither needed nor supported. Within that baseline,
three choices give this biosphere its signature:

| System | This world | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Heredity** | A double-stranded informational polymer, *the strand*, templated originally on vent clay-mineral surfaces. | Distinctive trait: it reads in **triplet-of-triplets** — a redundant, error-tolerant code, selected in the mutagenic high-radiation shallows before an ozone layer existed. |
| **Energy currency** | A phosphate-bond carrier, as universal here as ATP is on Earth. | Universality is the tell of single origin: *all* life shares it. |
| **Phototrophy** | **Two** pigment systems, and the split between them paints the planet. | See below — the single most visible fact about this world's life. |

### The two pigments, and why the sea is violet and the land is green

**INVENTED.** The **ancestral** light-harvesting pigment is a **retinal-type
molecule** (provisional: *ianin*). It is cheap, it works in the blue-green
light that penetrates seawater, and it absorbs the green–yellow band and
**reflects violet-magenta**. Because it arose first and the ocean is vast, it
was never displaced there: **the open sunlit ocean, in bloom, reads deep
violet.** MEASURED grounding — with 79 % of the planet ocean, the ancestral
pigment's domain *is* most of the planet's living surface.

A **derived** clade later evolved a **porphyrin-type pigment** (provisional:
*viror*) that absorbs red and blue and reflects **green**. It is more
efficient in the red-rich, unfiltered light of shallow water and open air, so
it dominates the coasts, the shelves, and — when life finally crawls ashore —
the **land**. Hence the planetary palette: **violet seas, green shores and
continents, and a permanent evolutionary tension** between the old cheap ocean
pigment and the young efficient land pigment wherever the two meet in the
tidal zone.

Both phototroph families **oxygenate** the water and air as they spread. The
oxygen they release is the precondition for large, active animals (§6) and is
consistent with the breathable, Earth-like atmosphere the climate model
assumes.

## 3. The deep root: three ancestral stocks

**INVENTED.** Below every plant, animal, and person lie three primordial
domains, split at the very base of the tree — the equivalent of Earth's
bacteria/archaea/eukaryote division, but drawn along this world's own history:

- **Ferralith** — the vent-rooted chemolithotrophs. The oldest stock, still
  living at the ridges, in back-arc springs, and kilometres down in the warm
  wet crust (the **deep biosphere**). Never left home; needs no sun. Everything
  else is a specialised offshoot of this.
- **Photera** — the stock that rose into the sunlit water column and invented
  phototrophy (the *ianin* pigment). The primary producers of the open sea;
  the base of the marine food web to this day.
- **Symbia** — the **complex-cell** lineage, born of endosymbiosis (§4). The
  ancestor of all "large" life: every multicellular alga, animal, fungus-analog
  and, eventually, every person is a Symbian.

---

## 4. The complex cell — one improbable merger

**INVENTED, dated by INTERPRETED climate.** A single **endosymbiotic event** —
a host cell that engulfed but did not digest a *ferralith* respirer, and later
a *photera* phototroph — produced the first complex cell, the founder of
**Symbia**. This is this world's "eukaryogenesis": rare, singular, and the
gateway to everything big.

Placed in the deep pre-icehouse ocean, well before the dated record opens.
Complex cells existed, diversified slowly, and were **already present when the
750-Myr record begins** — but stayed small, single-celled or loosely colonial,
for a very long time. The trigger that finally made them *big* was climatic,
and the record captures it exactly (§5).

## 5. The bottleneck: the S1 icehouse (T-650 … T-550)

**MEASURED.** The supercontinent **S1** assembled by T-650 Myr; with
subduction-ridge outgassing at a minimum and fresh collisional sutures
weathering hard, the climate crashed to its coldest state of the whole record —
**global mean 9.6 °C, 17 % of all land under ice or tundra, at the T-600
glacial maximum**.

**INTERPRETED / INVENTED.** This was the great **filter**. It was a hard cold,
not a total freeze (the tropics and the whole deep sea stayed liquid), so it
culled without resetting. Surface and shallow-water life was hammered; the
survivors were the lineages that could **wait in the deep and the tropical
refugia** — exactly the *ferralith* deep biosphere and the hardiest *Symbia*.
Cold-ocean, low-light, high-oxygen selection during this long squeeze
pre-adapted the complex-cell stock for what came next: it favoured larger
cells, stored reserves, and the first true **tissues**. Life came out of the
S1 icehouse smaller in variety but primed to explode.

## 6. The radiation: the Breakup Hothouse (T-550 … T-350)

**MEASURED.** S1 rifted apart. The LIP series **L1–L5** erupted in sequence and
thousands of kilometres of new mid-ocean ridge (Central, Western, Northern
oceans) outgassed CO₂ far faster than the young margins could weather it. The
planet swung to its **warmest state — global mean ≈ 22.6 °C at T-350, ice-free
poles** — and, crucially, breakup manufactured **vast new shallow passive-margin
shelves** around every fragment.

**INVENTED (from the physics).** Warm, sunlit, oxygen-rich, nutrient-fed
shallow seas on brand-new continental shelves are the perfect cradle for big
life — and this is where the tree's crown groups burst into being. In this
window arise the **founding multicellular kingdoms**, all Symbian:

| Founding kingdom (provisional) | Role | Descends to |
|---|---|---|
| **Violet-weeds** (*Photera*-derived + *ianin*) | Open-ocean and deep-shelf primary producers | The planet's plankton and kelp-analog forests — the violet seas |
| **Green-flora** (*viror* clade) | Shallow-water then terrestrial producers | Every coastal alga and, later, all **land plants** — the green continents |
| **Reticans** (fungus/decomposer analog) | Recyclers, then the symbionts that make soil possible | Decomposer webs; the mycorrhiza-analog partners that let plants colonise land |
| **Zoa** (the animal stem) | Motile multicellular consumers | **All animals**, marine and terrestrial, up to the humanoid lineage |

The **Zoa** stem is the one to watch, because everything the culture layer will
care about is a Zoan. Its founding body plan (§8) is set here, in the hothouse
shelves, and it is already **skeletonised, oxygen-hungry, and bilaterally
mobile** by the end of the radiation.

## 7. Dispersal and the splitting of the world (T-350 … T-0)

**MEASURED.** After the hothouse the continents kept drifting apart and the
climate slowly cooled through the **Collisional-cooling** and **Modern-icehouse**
eras into the present. By T-0 the world is four dispersed continents separated
by broad oceans.

**INTERPRETED — this is the hand-off to the realms layer.** Because the deep
tree (everything through §6) is **older than the breakup**, all four continents
inherit the *same* founding stocks — the same kingdoms, the same Zoan body
plan, the same two pigments. What differs is everything that evolved *after*
they separated. The geological record gives the exact order of separation
(mapping the lettered cratons onto the modern continents):

```
              ┌── Meridia   (cratons A·I·J — west flank)
      ┌───────┤
      │       └── Sirocca   (cratons C·E·F — west flank)
──────┤                                   split ~T-400
      │       ┌── Borea     (craton  G     — core, cast off NE)
      └───────┤
              └── Selvana   (cratons B·D·H — core)
                                          split ~T-400
   deepest split ~T-450: {west flank} | {core}
```

**The load-bearing hook:** the continents that are **biological sisters** are
*not* the continents that are **easy to sail between**. Meridia and Sirocca
share the deepest ancestry (both west-flank), yet today sit far apart; Meridia
and Selvana are near neighbours across the warm Equatorial Western Sea (easy
contact) yet belong to **different halves of the tree**. Every realm, every
"why does this creature exist here and nowhere else," and every strange
cross-ocean parallel the peoples will one day puzzle over is seeded by this
mismatch between **kinship and reachability**. That is the subject of the next
document.

## 8. Signatures every descendant carries

**INVENTED.** A short list of ancestral traits, fixed deep in the tree, that
make this world's life recognisably *itself* — the through-lines a regional
designer should keep true across all four continents:

- **Violet base, green crown.** Any primary producer is either an old
  violet (*ianin*) type or a derived green (*viror*) type; the sea skews violet,
  the land skews green, the tidal zone is where they war.
- **The Zoan body plan.** The animal stem is **bilaterian with an internal
  skeleton and a closed circulation**; the ancestral respiratory pigment is
  **copper-based and blue** (efficient in cold, oxygen-rich seas), with an
  iron-red variant derived later in the most active warm-blooded lineages. Blue
  blood is ancestral here; red blood is the specialisation.
- **Triplet-of-triplets heredity.** A redundant, error-tolerant genetic code —
  biologically, life here mutates and speciates a little more slowly and
  conservatively than Earth life, which is why deep body plans persist across
  the whole 750-Myr record.
- **A deep biosphere that never died.** The *ferralith* stock still lives in
  the crust and at the vents on every plate; it is the planet's backup copy of
  life and a permanent chemical engine under the sea floor.
