# Life Through Deep Time — a paleobiology of the 750-Myr record

*Planet `06cy8w6z6a89kow6psje93` · how the single tree of life became the four
realms, told forward across the same deep-time record as the rock and the
climate.*

This document runs the biology **forward through time**, era by era, along the
exact clock of the geological and climate record
([`../GEOLOGICAL_HISTORY.md`](../GEOLOGICAL_HISTORY.md),
[`../PALEOCLIMATE.md`](../PALEOCLIMATE.md)). It is the causal bridge between the
two present-tense documents already written: it starts from the founding tree
([`00_TREE_OF_LIFE.md`](00_TREE_OF_LIFE.md)) and ends, at T-0, on exactly the
realm map ([`01_BIOGEOGRAPHIC_REALMS.md`](01_BIOGEOGRAPHIC_REALMS.md)) — showing
*how* the shared biota came to be partitioned the way it is.

### One caveat that changes how to read this

The geological history could be told forward because it was **computed** — plate
motions integrated over time, climate forced by dated events and validated
against the present. **Biology has no such engine.** There is no fossil record
in the dataset; every past organism here is **INVENTED**. So this is not a
reconstruction dressed as one: it is invented narrative **pinned to** the
measured climate and geography of each era, never derived from data. It is
structured by the paleoclimate's **six eras** (not a uniform stage grid) because
biological change is uneven — it clusters at a few real inflection points, and
the era sections mark those, the way the geology tables mark real tectonic
events rather than filler.

Because it is invented throughout, this document **constrains nothing** the way
the physical canon does. It is the connective tissue — the deep-time substrate
for later myths, and the causal justification for the realms. Labels as
elsewhere: **MEASURED** / **INTERPRETED** / **INVENTED**.

## §0 · Deep prelude — before the record (older than T-750)

The origin of life and everything up to the first complex cell happened in the
deep past, long before the 750-Myr window opens (doc 00 §1–4). By the time the
record begins, the groundwork is already laid:

- Life rooted at the hydrothermal vents (the *ferralith* stock) and rose into
  the water column, inventing phototrophy with the ancestral violet *ianin*
  pigment (the *photera* stock).
- Those phototrophs **oxygenated the sea and then the air** across deep time —
  the slow great oxygenation, complete well before T-750, without which none of
  what follows is possible.
- A single endosymbiosis produced the complex cell (**Symbia**).

**The state of the world at T-750:** oxygenated violet seas full of microbial
mats and single-celled or loosely-colonial complex life — but **nothing large,
no true tissues, and no land life at all.** The stage is set; the actors are
small.

## §1 · The Priming — Assembly cool-down (T-750 … T-650)

**Physical state (MEASURED / INTERPRETED).** The ten cratons converge and dock
into supercontinent S1; the pre-cycle oceans close, opening shallow
epicontinental seas along the new sutures. Climate slides from mild toward cold
(16.9 °C → 12.9 °C).

**Biology (INVENTED).** The assembling shallow seas and the cooling together
select for the first experiments in **true multicellularity**: the earliest
tissue-grade Symbians — soft-bodied, benthic, still marine-only. Habitat is
being reshaped (oceans closing, interiors drying), which pressures the plankton
and rewards the new bottom-dwelling forms. This is the priming of the tree: the
first bodies, no skeletons yet.

| Biological event | Type | Physical driver |
|---|---|---|
| First tissue-grade multicellular Symbians (benthic, soft-bodied) | origination | new epicontinental shelf seas along S1 sutures |
| Plankton stress; benthic forms favoured | turnover | ocean closure + cooling |

## §2 · The Filter — S1 icehouse (T-650 … T-550)

**Physical state (MEASURED).** S1 complete and ringed by trenches; its southern
flank sits past 55 °S; outgassing at minimum, weathering high. The climate
crashes to the coldest of the whole record — **9.6 °C and 17 % land ice at the
T-600 glacial maximum**. A hard cold, but not a total freeze: tropics and the
whole deep sea stay liquid.

**Biology (INVENTED).** The great **bottleneck**. Surface and shallow life is
culled; the survivors are those that can wait in the two refuges the physics
leaves open — the deep sea (the *ferralith*-rooted hadal biosphere) and the
tropical shallows. Cold, dark, oxygen-rich selection over ~100 Myr favours
larger cells, stored reserves, and the consolidation of **true tissues**. The
tree comes out of the icehouse **poorer in variety but primed** — the four
founding kingdom stems survive in refugia, ready. This is the record's first
mass extinction (**E1**).

| Biological event | Type | Physical driver |
|---|---|---|
| **E1** — the icehouse extinction: shallow/surface diversity crash | extinction | T-600 glacial maximum |
| Refugial survival in deep sea + tropics | survival | permanent liquid refuges |
| Consolidation of the four founding kingdom stems; first true tissues | origination | cold/high-O₂ selection |

## §3 · The Radiation — Breakup hothouse (T-550 … T-350)

**Physical state (MEASURED).** S1 rifts apart: L1 (T-550) through L5 (T-350)
erupt in sequence, three young oceans (Central, Western, Northern) open, and
breakup manufactures **vast new shallow passive-margin shelves** around every
fragment. The planet swings to its **warmest state — 22.6 °C, ice-free poles**.
Vicariance begins: the T-450 rift splits west-flank from core, and T-400 splits
Meridia | Sirocca and Borea | Selvana.

**Biology (INVENTED).** The **great radiation** — the hinge of the whole tree.
Warm, sunlit, oxygen-rich, nutrient-fed shelves are the perfect cradle, and the
primed survivors explode across them. Three things happen at once:

1. **The crown groups burst into being.** The four founding kingdoms —
   violet-weeds, green-flora, reticans, and the **Zoa** animal stem —
   diversify massively. Skeletons appear in the Zoa (the *skeletal revolution*);
   bilaterian, mobile, blue-blooded predators arrive and an arms race begins.
2. **Life goes ashore.** As green-flora and reticans partner (the mycorrhiza-
   analog that first makes soil), plants creep onto the wet new coasts, then
   inland along rivers; the first land Zoa follow the plants as detritivores.
   The continents turn green for the first time.
3. **The last shared inheritance is sundered.** These hothouse crown groups are
   the final biota all four continents hold in common — and the very breakup
   that created their cradle begins to **cut it apart** (T-450, then T-400).
   Everything after this evolves separately per fragment.

Extinction is muted here — the LIP series causes pulsed disturbances, but in a
warm, opportunity-rich world these drive **turnover, not collapse**.

| Biological event | Type | Physical driver |
|---|---|---|
| Radiation of the four founding kingdoms | radiation | new warm passive-margin shelves |
| The skeletal revolution; bilaterian predators; the arms race | origination | shelf productivity + O₂ |
| **First land colonization** (green-flora + reticans → soil; first land Zoa) | colonization | wet rifted coasts, ice-free warmth |
| Vicariance begins: west-flank \| core, then the sister splits | vicariance | breakup T-450 / T-400 |
| LIP-pulse turnover (L1–L5) | turnover | the LIP series |

## §4 · The Sundering — Dispersal moderation (T-350 … T-250)

**Physical state (MEASURED).** The LIP era ends and the fragments disperse in
earnest; leading edges harden into volcanic arcs, trailing margins accumulate
shelf sediment, and the young oceans widen. Climate is warm but moderating
(22.6 °C → 20.1 °C).

**Biology (INVENTED).** For the first time the continents **radiate
independently**, and the realms begin to take on distinct character. Land life
matures — the first true **green-flora forests** and a diversifying land Zoa
fauna on each fragment, each drawn from the shared hothouse stock but now going
its own way. In the sea, the split the marine realms record becomes real: the
**young rift-oceans** (Central, Western, Northern) fill with derived faunas
colonised from the shelves, while the **ancient EXT** keeps its old endemic
pelagic lineages. Widening water deepens every isolation.

| Biological event | Type | Physical driver |
|---|---|---|
| Independent continental radiations begin | provincialization | dispersal + widening oceans |
| First mature terrestrial forests | radiation | warm, greening continents |
| Young-ocean faunas diverge from old-EXT endemics | provincialization | new ocean basins vs ancient EXT |

## §5 · The Provincing — Collisional cooling (T-250 … T-50)

**Physical state (MEASURED).** The great drawdown. O4 flat-slab uplift on Borea
(T-250), the O1 collision that assembles Meridia (T-200), C's sustained Andean
belt on Sirocca (O3), and the O2 collision that reassembles Selvana (T-100)
expose fresh rock and pull CO₂ down for 200 Myr; far-southern frost returns by
T-150 and polar ice locks in by T-100. Hotspot chains H1–H15 light up (T-100).

**Biology (INVENTED).** Cooling **and** mountain-building carve the
**intra-continental provinces** of the realm map. As the great walls rise (O1–O6),
their leeward rain shadows open the continental **deserts**, and their flanks
open **alpine** zones — the exact provinces of doc 01. Two lineages of first
importance are set here:

- **The deserts, and the homology puzzle they encode.** A drought-tolerant
  xeric stem had already arisen in the seasonally-dry interiors of the
  west-flank biota *before* the T-400 Meridia–Sirocca split — so it is **shared
  inheritance**. When each of those continents later built its own rain-shadow
  desert (Meridia behind the O1 front, Sirocca behind O3), that shared stem
  **radiated in parallel** into full desert forms on both. Hence doc 01's rule:
  Meridia's and Sirocca's deserts are **homologous** (one west-flank stem, split
  by an ocean), while Selvana's Interior Dry Basin — built from the *core*
  branch's own stock behind O2 — is merely **convergent**. The historical spine
  is what makes that a *result*, not an assertion.
- **The cold specialists.** The returning poles and the new high ground select
  the cold- and seasonal-adapted lineages that will define Borea and the
  far-southern margins — the ancestral blue blood of the Zoan plan (doc 00 §8)
  paying off in exactly the cold it was first tuned to.

The hotspot chains begin generating **island endemics** — the sweepstakes engine
of doc 01 §5 switches on. Cooling and the drying of interiors drive a moderate
turnover (**E2**) as habitats are lost.

| Biological event | Type | Physical driver |
|---|---|---|
| Alpine biotas open on the rising walls | radiation | O1–O6 orogeny |
| Parallel desert radiations from the shared west-flank xeric stem | radiation | leeward rain shadows behind new orogens |
| Selvana's convergent desert biota | radiation | O2 rain shadow, core-branch stock |
| Cold-/seasonal-specialist lineages established | radiation | returning poles, cooling |
| Island-endemic radiations begin | provincialization | hotspot chains H1–H15 |
| **E2** — drawdown turnover on drying interiors | extinction | collisional cooling |

## §6 · The Present Cross-section — Modern icehouse (T-50 … T-0)

**Physical state (MEASURED).** The modern configuration: O5 (Meridia's 8.5 km
cordillera) and O6 (Borea) rise to the planet's highest ground, the young oceans
take their first trenches, four dispersed continents stand in a moderately cold
world with permanent far-southern polar ice (present global mean 17.2 °C, 9 %
land ice+tundra).

**Biology (INVENTED).** The living world settles into **exactly the realm map**
of doc 01. The highest new ground (O5, and the equatorial sky-island ranges)
sharpens the most extreme alpine and endemic provinces; cold specialists hold
Borea and the far south; the interchange fronts — above all the Equatorial
Western Sea — operate as the only crossings. Somewhere in this era, in one
realm's Zoan fauna, sits the lineage that will lead to the peoples of Phase 5:
the last open question of the life layer, and the subject of the humanoid-
ancestry document. T-0 is the present, and the present is the realms.

## Summary — the biological clock

| Era | T-range (Myr) | Climate (global mean) | Biological state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deep prelude | > 750 | — | Origin, oxygenation, complex cells; small marine life only |
| The Priming | 750 → 650 | 16.9 → 12.9 °C | First true multicellular tissues (marine) |
| The Filter | 650 → 550 | **9.6 °C** min | Icehouse bottleneck (**E1**); founding stems primed in refugia |
| The Radiation | 550 → 350 | up to **22.6 °C** | Four kingdoms explode; skeletons; **land colonised**; vicariance begins |
| The Sundering | 350 → 250 | 22.6 → 20.1 °C | Independent radiations begin; forests; old-EXT vs young-ocean seas |
| The Provincing | 250 → 50 | 20.1 → 16.4 °C | Mountains + deserts + cold zones make the provinces (**E2**); islands |
| Present Cross-section | 50 → 0 | 16.4 → 17.2 °C | The realm map; the humanoid lineage exists |

**The extinction record:** **E1**, the S1 icehouse filter (T-600) — the deep
bottleneck that shaped the whole tree; muted LIP-pulse turnover through the
breakup hothouse; **E2**, the collisional-drawdown turnover (T-150 → T-100) as
the poles refroze. Two true crises, both climate-driven, both tied to a dated
forcing — the same discipline as the geology's event record.
