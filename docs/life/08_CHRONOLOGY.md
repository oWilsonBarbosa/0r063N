# The Chronology — dating the peoples

*Planet `06cy8w6z6a89kow6psje93` · how a world whose record has a 50-Myr grid
gets a history measured in thousands of years — and what that history turns out
to be.*

Docs 00–07 built a biosphere on a record whose finest resolution is **50 Myr**.
That was enough for everything up to now: the founding tree, the realm map, the
deep-time paleobiology, the regional ecologies. It is not enough for the one
thing the culture layer actually needs, which is *when*.

This document builds the clock first and the history second. The order matters.
An invented chronology is a list of dates someone liked; a **derived** one is a
list of consequences of a single stated parameter, and can be argued with,
rescaled, or rejected as a whole.

### Labels, and the one free parameter

**MEASURED** / **INTERPRETED** / **INVENTED** as elsewhere. This document has a
higher INVENTED fraction than any before it, and it concentrates that invention
deliberately into **one number** — the *basin cycle* of §3. Every date below is
stated both in years and in cycles, so changing that one number rescales the
whole chronology mechanically instead of breaking it.

Nothing here changes any MEASURED value. The physical canon is untouched.

---

## 1. Why the 50-Myr grid cannot carry this

Doc 03 states the problem in its own resolution caveat:

> The deep-time record's finest grid is 50 Myr. This document is pinned to that
> grid at exactly two points — the onset of the O5 orogeny at T-50, and the
> present at T-0. Everything between them is INVENTED at a resolution the
> physical record does not possess.

That is honest but it leaves the entire origin of the peoples as a single
undivided 50-Myr block. Every question the culture layer wants to ask — how long
have they been in Selvana, is Borea older or younger than agriculture, how deep
is the difference between two Meridian peoples — lives *inside* that block and
cannot be answered from it.

**INTERPRETED.** But the record's coarseness is a property of the *global*
reconstruction, not of the cradle. And the cradle, as docs 03 and 04 established
it, happens to be the single most datable landscape on the planet:

- an **aulacogen** — a subsiding trough that accumulates sediment continuously
  instead of eroding it away;
- an **active volcanic field** inside it (plume P5, continental hotspots
  **H13–H15**, at `[28, −90]`);
- and **three great lakes at three different depths**, in one basin system.

A rift trough that is simultaneously accumulating sediment, receiving ash falls,
and holding lakes of contrasting sensitivity is not a landscape you have to
guess at. It is a landscape that writes things down.

## 2. Five clocks

**INTERPRETED throughout** — every underlying fact is MEASURED and already
published; what is interpreted is their use as chronometers.

### 2.1 Tephra — the absolute clock

Plume **P5** lit up beneath the AU1 rift scar at T-100, driving continental
hotspots **H13–H15** under eastern AIJ at `[28, −90]` — inside the cradle box
(15–28 °N, 110–85 °W). An active volcanic field interbedding ash into lake
sediment gives **instantaneous, geochemically distinguishable horizons**
stacked through the whole section.

This is the backbone. Everything else in this section is a way of counting
between ash falls.

### 2.2 The three lakes — three recorders, three sensitivities

Doc 04 §2 already assigned these their ecological roles. They double as clocks,
and the depths that decide the ecology decide the chronometry too:

| Lake | Depth | Doc 04's role | As a clock |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **The Deep** | **821 m** at 2,090 m elev. | "the refuge that never fails" | **The master reference.** Cold, oligotrophic, permanent, never empties — so its section is *continuous*. This is the planet's ice core. |
| **The Shallow** | **36 m** | "the pump… this is where the speciation pump physically runs" | **The cycle counter.** Fills and empties on the basin cycle, so its section is a stack of wet/dry couplets — one per cycle, countable. |
| **The Sump** | 198 m, no outlet | "terminal, concentrating… saline and getting more so" | **The ratchet.** Salinity only ever increases. Not a cycle counter but a **monotonic** one — it says *how far through*, never *which cycle*. |

The division of labour is exact: The Shallow counts cycles, The Deep spans them
without gaps, The Sump says which direction time runs.

One consequence to hold on to, because §4 turns on it: **The Shallow stops
recording at the moment it stops filling.** The pump's own recorder switches off
precisely when the pump fails. Everything after that has to be read off The Deep.

### 2.3 Hotspot chains — dating the crossings

Doc 01 §5 makes the **hotspot chains H1–H15 age-progressive** and names them
sweepstakes routes: "each chain a chronosequence of colonisation." Age-progressive
means distance along a chain *is* absolute age, which makes these the one clock
that dates events out on the water rather than in the cradle — that is, the one
that can date a **crossing**. An island that had not yet been built cannot have
been a stepping-stone.

### 2.4 O5 uplift — the arrow

Cyclic records are ambiguous: any single wet/dry couplet looks like any other.
Something has to say which cycle you are in.

**MEASURED.** O5 is `T-50..now`, "still rising," 7.53 km max, and one of four
belts (O3, O5, O6, O7) the erosion model flags **exempt from decay because they
are still building** — the largest of them at 1,540 m mean, and the only one on
Meridia at all (O3 is Selvanan, O6 Borean, O7 an intra-oceanic arc on
`micro_7`). Its rain shadow therefore deepens monotonically
across the entire span this document covers. Every basin cycle is drier than the
one before it. That converts an ambiguous stack of couplets into an ordered one.

### 2.5 Ice volume — the crossing gate

**MEASURED.** The last three stages run **7 % → 8 % → 9 %** land ice and tundra
while global mean temperature moves only 16.2 → 16.4 → 17.2 °C. Doc 03 §3 reads
this correctly: a world with permanent ice and a flat mean **oscillates rather
than drifts**.

Oscillating ice means oscillating sea level, which means the sea crossings of
doc 01 §5 are **not available continuously**. They open at lowstands and close
at highstands. Note that T-0 is both the warmest *and* the iciest of the last
three stages — the present is a highstand at peak ice extent, so **the crossings
happened when the water was lower than it is now**, and the routes the peoples
actually used are partly underwater today.

This is the clock that makes dispersal dates *derived* rather than chosen.

### Summary

| Clock | Kind | Dates | Resolves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tephra (P5 · H13–H15) | absolute horizons | anything in the cradle | a single ash fall |
| The Deep | continuous section | the whole span | sub-cycle |
| The Shallow | cycle counter | the pump, to its failure | one basin cycle |
| The Sump | monotonic ratchet | long trends, direction | which direction, not which cycle |
| Hotspot chains H1–H15 | age-progressive | crossings, island availability | chain segment |
| O5 uplift | monotonic arrow | disambiguates the couplets | long trends |
| Ice volume → sea level | oscillating gate | crossing windows | one cycle |

## 3. The basin cycle — the one free parameter

**INVENTED, and deliberately isolated here.** Doc 03 §3 invokes an orbital
oscillation without giving it a period, and the SKILL's open list puts
"calendar, day length, moon system, gravity" entirely in Phase-5 creative space.
So the period is ours to set, and it should be set **once, explicitly**.

> **The basin cycle** — the dominant period of the lake-level oscillation in the
> AU1 system. Proposed value: **100 kyr.**

Two notes on it. It is the period of the *lake's response*, not necessarily of a
raw orbital term — on Earth the ~100-kyr late-Pleistocene rhythm is a nonlinear
response to a much weaker eccentricity forcing, and nothing here requires a
tidier story than that. And it is the only place a reader has to accept a number
on faith: shorten it and the pump becomes more aggressive and the peoples
younger in cycles; lengthen it and the reverse. Every figure below carries its
cycle count so the rescaling is arithmetic.

## 4. Two one-way trends, two events

**INTERPRETED, and this is the argument of the document.**

Doc 03 §6 established that the pump *never completed a split* — every arid phase
started a divergence and every wet phase reabsorbed it, for the whole modern
icehouse. What §6 never asked is the obvious next question: **the pump is still
described in the present tense, so why are the peoples here at all?**

The answer is that two of the cradle's trends run one way only, and each of them
eventually crosses a threshold.

### The Failure — the pump stops (~T-800 kyr · 8,000 cycles)

The pump physically runs on **The Shallow**, 36 m deep, filling and emptying.
Its refill depends on a rain regime that O5's rain shadow has been drying
monotonically since T-50. At some cycle, the refill does not come — and then does
not come again.

That is the end of a 49.2-Myr process, and it does something the pump had spent
that entire time preventing. The isolates on the shoulders and the volcanic highs
lose their reconnection mechanism, but they also lose the ground between them:
the drying basin pushes them **off the middle and onto the two waters that
remain**, 1,843 m apart in the vertical —

- **The Sump**, low, saline, hostile, and productive at its margins;
- **The Deep**, high, cold, oligotrophic, poor, and absolutely reliable.

Only a lineage that can work salt *and* altitude can use the whole system. Doc 03
§5 already lists both as ancestral Aulacine equipment, motivated by MEASURED
facts — "304 mm/yr, 65 % of it in one half-year, beside a **salt** sea" and
"Meridia is 18.2 % above 2 km… the lineage was never a lowland specialist."

**The Failure does not invent that body. It makes it decisive.** This is exactly
the move doc 03 §5 already makes for red blood — "the trait was available; the
heat is what made it win" — applied to the two traits that were sitting there
unexplained.

What comes out of the two refuges is one interbreeding population carrying the
recombined variation of 49.2 Myr of pumping. **That is the crown Aulacine.**

### Why this is a fusion and not a bottleneck

This matters, because the staging looks like Earth's and the genetics are the
reverse.

A bottleneck **reduces** effective population size and diversity, and its
signature downstream is a serial founder effect — each further population poorer
than the last. The Failure does the opposite: it takes many long-separated
isolates and **merges** them. Between-population variation converts into
within-population variation. Total diversity is roughly conserved; what changes
is where it is stored.

So the expansion that follows starts from **the most diverse population the
lineage has ever held**, not from a surviving remnant. Three consequences, and
the culture layer should hold all three:

- **The founding population is not a small band.** Any origin story that runs on
  "a handful of survivors" is reading this backwards.
- **Regional variation among the peoples is not drift after dispersal.** It is
  ancestral variation being re-sorted — which is why it can be *older* than the
  populations carrying it.
- **Doc 03 §6's "almost-cousins" now have an address.** The deeply divergent
  populations it promised are the pre-Failure isolates: the ones that reached a
  refuge late, or reached the wrong one, or reached neither.

### The Surge — the basin becomes unusable (~T-200 kyr · 2,000 cycles)

The second one-way trend is **The Sump's salinity**. Doc 04 states it plainly:
no outlet, water leaves only by evaporation, so it is "saline and getting more
so." That is a ratchet, and ratchets end somewhere.

When the Sump's margins cross from *hostile* to *unusable*, the richer of the two
refuges is gone. What remains is The Deep — permanent, reliable, and
oligotrophic, which is to say safe and poor. A population held in one basin for
49 Myr by a pump that kept reeling it back in is now in a basin that no longer
feeds it.

**The Surge is an expulsion, not an achievement.** That is the answer to the
question this chronology exists to answer — why a lineage that stayed in one
trough for tens of millions of years covers a planet in the last two hundred
thousand years. Nothing about them changed first. The basin stopped working.

The behavioural package doc 03 leaves open — fire, language, the technology that
makes Borea reachable — assembles **after** this point and under its pressure,
across roughly 2,000 cycles. The ratio of biological to behavioural modernity is
about 4:1 (800 kyr : 200 kyr), which is close to Earth's own ~300 : 70 kyr. That
is a proportion, not a copy: it lands there because the same kind of gap exists,
not because it was matched.

## 5. The chronology

**INVENTED**, derived from §3's one parameter and gated by §2's clocks.

| | Date | Cycles BP | Gate |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Aulacine grade** (doc 03 §4) | T-50 Myr | 500,000 | O5 rises; the forest fragments |
| — the pump runs, and never completes a split — | | | |
| **The Failure** · the crown Aulacine | **~800 kyr** | **8,000** | The Shallow's last drying |
| **The Surge** · out of the basin | **~200 kyr** | **2,000** | The Sump goes unusable |
| Across Meridia · M3 → M4 | ~160 kyr | 1,600 | the ecotone; NPP 537 → 1353 |
| **Selvana** | **~90 kyr** | **900** | Equatorial Western Sea — warm, narrow, the R3 rift itself |
| **Sirocca** | **~30 kyr** | **300** | the Eastern Ocean — widest barrier, only cross-branch route; needs a deep lowstand, the H-chains, and real seafaring |
| **Borea**, onto **B1** | **~10 kyr** | **100** | the Eastern tropical front; deliberate and technological |

The ordering is not new — doc 03 §7 fixed it as *near-and-kin → far-and-alien →
far-alien-and-cold*. What is new is the **spacing**, and the spacing is doing
work: each crossing is an order of magnitude rarer than the last, and the gaps
between them are long enough to contain the technological difference doc 03
insists on when it calls Borea "a late, deliberate, technologically-mediated
arrival, not a dispersal."

### Divergence depths, and the inversion

| Separation | Depth |
|---|---:|
| Ancestral Meridian variation (pre-Failure, carried through the fusion) | up to **49.2 Myr** |
| Deepest genealogy *within* the crown species | **~800 kyr** |
| Meridia ↔ Selvana | ~90 kyr |
| Meridia ↔ Sirocca | ~30 kyr |
| Meridia ↔ Borea | ~10 kyr |

**This is the shape that distinguishes this world from Earth's.** On Earth the
deepest human divergence is African vs. non-African, and the homeland's internal
depth exceeds the deepest continental split by roughly 5×. Here it exceeds it by
**9×** on genealogy and by **550×** on ancestral variation.

Stated for use: **two Meridian peoples can be more distantly related to each
other than either is to anyone in Borea.** Regional difference is ancient;
continental difference is a recent twig. Doc 03 §6 asserted this without a
number; this document supplies one.

## 6. What this changes upstream

**Doc 03 §4's tree.** One number moves. The Aulacine grade is extended to the
Failure and the crown species begins there:

```
└── Aulacines  (T-50…T-800 kyr, the AU1 trough)
     └── the crown Aulacine  (T-800 kyr…T-0)
```

The previously published boundary was **T-8 Myr**, which put an anatomically
modern, endurance-adapted, tool-using forager in one continent for 8 Myr without
leaving it. The pump explains a long delay but not one that long, and 8 Myr also
sits outside the resolution the clocks of §2 can deliver — the entire history
would have been undatable by its own landscape. At 800 kyr the whole species
history fits inside one continuous section of The Deep.

**Doc 03 §6 gains its close.** Everything §6 says stays true: the pump ran for
tens of millions of years, never completed a split, and produced exactly one
sapient species with ancient internal structure and no second kind of person.
What it lacked was an ending. It now has one — *the pump never split, and then it
stopped, and the stopping is what made the peoples.*

**What does not change.** The two "fixed" spines — doc 03 §9 and the SKILL —
assert the origin window as **T-50 → T-0** and the lineage as
`Zoa → Thermozoa → Dendrozoa → Aulacines → crown Aulacine`. Neither moves. Nor
does the cradle, the body, the dispersal order, the red-blood/west-flank result,
or any figure in docs 04–07.

## 7. What this fixes, and what comes next

**Fixed (the chronology spine):**

- The cradle is **datable by its own landscape**: tephra from P5's H13–H15 field
  for absolute horizons, The Shallow as a cycle counter, The Deep as a continuous
  reference, The Sump as a monotonic ratchet, O5's uplift as the arrow, and
  hotspot chains H1–H15 plus sea level as the gate on crossings.
- One free parameter, the **basin cycle**, proposed at **100 kyr**. Every date is
  also given in cycles so the whole chronology rescales from that one number.
- **The Failure (~800 kyr)** — The Shallow's last drying ends the pump and fuses
  its isolates on the two surviving waters. This is the origin of the crown
  Aulacine, and it is a **fusion, not a bottleneck**: the founding population is
  the most diverse the planet has held.
- **The Surge (~200 kyr)** — the Sump's salinity ratchet makes the basin
  unusable. Dispersal is an **expulsion**, not an achievement.
- Dispersal: **Selvana ~90 kyr · Sirocca ~30 kyr · Borea ~10 kyr**, each gated by
  a sea-level lowstand and each an order of magnitude harder than the last.
- **Regional difference is older than continental difference** — by 9× on
  genealogy, 550× on ancestral variation. The inverse of Earth's shape.

**Open:**

- **Borea at ~10 kyr lands on top of the agricultural window.** Doc 07 gives B1
  an independent cereal cradle in **Winterseed**; on this chronology that cradle
  is roughly *contemporaneous with arrival* rather than long predating it — so
  Borea was farmed almost as soon as it was peopled, by arrivals who brought the
  idea of farming and not one of their crops. That is a large claim for the
  culture layer and it should be checked against doc 07 §5 before it is built on.
- **The behavioural package is still unwritten.** This document dates the Surge
  and says what pushed it; it does not say what fire, language, or seafaring
  looked like, or in what order they arrived across the 2,000 cycles.
  [`09`](09_THE_MERIDIAN_INTERVAL.md) has since occupied the interval — the
  600 kyr in the basin and the 110 kyr across the continent — and narrowed the
  seafaring question sharply: the Selvanan crossing needs only **repeatable
  short-water craft** (75 km as the longest leg, island-hopped), not blue-water
  capability. That is still owed for Sirocca at ~30 kyr.
- **The relict populations have dates now but no ecology.** Doc 07 §4 predicts
  B1 is Borea's endemism reservoir; the same logic applied to the pre-Failure
  isolates of the cradle predicts Meridian relict *peoples*, and the deep-time
  record has not been asked about them.
- **The basin cycle is unvalidated.** 100 kyr is a proposal, not a derivation.
  If the moon system and orbit are ever fixed in Phase 5, this number should be
  re-derived from them rather than kept.
