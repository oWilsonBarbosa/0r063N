# The Meridian Interval — 710,000 years before the crossing

*Planet `06cy8w6z6a89kow6psje93` · what the peoples did with the 89 % of their
existence they spent on one continent, and the road out that the data turned out
to be hiding.*

Doc 08 dated the peoples but did not occupy them. Between the Failure at
~800 kyr and the Selvanan crossing at ~90 kyr lies an interval that is **89 % of
the crown species' existence**, and nothing in the layer says a word about it.

This document fills that interval. It is the most INVENTED document in the life
layer — and it turns on four MEASURED results that were not known when doc 08
was written, one of which corrects a published claim in doc 01.

### Labels and reproduction

**MEASURED** / **INTERPRETED** / **INVENTED** as elsewhere. Dates are doc 08's
and inherit its one free parameter (the basin cycle, 100 kyr). Distances are
great-circle on the corrected connected-landmass partition; the cradle profile
reproduces doc 04's.

The measured results of §3 come from a new tool,
[`tools/crossings/`](../../tools/crossings/main.mjs), which describes the water
between landmasses rather than the land itself — the gap, the stepping-stone
route, and whether two points are joined by land under a height ceiling.

```sh
node tools/province-ecology/main.mjs M3 --box 15,28,-110,-85 --label cradle
node tools/crossings/main.mjs --pair Meridia,Selvana --hops --frame 20,36,-160,-140
node tools/crossings/main.mjs --pair Meridia,Selvana --corridor \
    --from 21,-97 --to 27.5,-148.4 --band 20,34
node tools/crossings/main.mjs --pair Meridia,Selvana --shore 25,30,-152,-144 --shore-only
```

## 2. The long basin — T-800 to T-200 kyr

**INVENTED**, from the regime doc 08 §4 leaves behind.

The Failure does not hand them a paradise. It hands them a **three-part basin
with its middle removed**. The Shallow — 33,200 km², the thing that had been
filling and emptying for 49 Myr — is now a dry pan, and what remains is two
waters **1,362 km and 1,843 m apart**:

| | Elevation | Depth | What it offers |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **The Sump** | 247 m | 198 m | rich margins, permanent, **saline and salinifying** |
| **The Shallow** *(pan)* | 287 m | — | 33,200 km² of flat dry ground that did not exist before |
| **The Deep** | **2,090 m** | 821 m | fresh, cold, oligotrophic, **absolutely reliable** |

### Transhumance is ancestral, not invented

Doc 08 §4 establishes that only a lineage able to work **salt and altitude
together** came through the Failure at all. That is not a fact about physiology
alone — it is a fact about *movement*. A population that must use a saline
lowland and a cold highland 1,362 km apart is a population with an **annual
circuit**, and it had one before it was a species.

This is the single most important thing to carry out of this document. Every
later Meridian people inherits a body and a habit built for **long, regular,
vertical seasonal movement between two unlike places**. Sedentism, when it
comes, will be the novelty. Moving will not.

### The apprenticeship

Three things run continuously for the whole 600 kyr, and each is a MEASURED
feature of the cradle rather than a convenience:

- **Stone that never runs out.** Plume P5's hotspots H13–H15 sit inside the
  cradle box at `[28, −90]`. Doc 03 §4 already has the Aulacines working "stone
  from a volcanic field that never stops producing fresh material." Six hundred
  thousand years of the same rock, on the same circuit, in the same hands.
- **The long acquaintance.** Doc 04's biota — **Flushgrass**, the **Rainherds**,
  the **Sun-tuber**, **Waterstem**, **Saltmat** — are not encountered here, they
  are *lived with*, for 6,000 cycles, before anything is domesticated. When the
  culture layer eventually domesticates Flushgrass and the Rainherd, it is
  formalising a relationship older than the species' dispersal.
- **The slow squeeze.** The Sump is salinifying the entire time. No generation
  can perceive it; every generation is nearer the end of it. The rich half of
  the system is dying on a schedule nobody in the basin can read.

**It is not an eden and it is not a trap. It is a very long, very stable
apprenticeship in a place that is quietly running out.**

## 3. The road out — four measured results

**MEASURED, and none of this was known when doc 08 was written.**

### 3.1 The throat is at 27.5 °N, not at the equator

Minimum great-circle distance from Meridian coast to Selvanan coast, by the
latitude of the Meridian shore:

| Meridian shore | Gap to Selvana |
|---|---:|
| −20 … 0 °N | 3,911 – 4,169 km |
| 0 … 10 °N | 2,572 km |
| 10 … 20 °N | 704 km |
| **20 … 30 °N** | **213 km** — *the throat*, at **27.5 °N, 148.4 °W** |
| 30 … 40 °N | 376 km |
| 40 … 60 °N | 1,420 – 2,444 km |

**The sea is widest at the equator and narrowest in the subtropics.** Doc 01 §5
calls it "the Equatorial Western Sea," which is true of the **R3 rift that
opened it** — R3 is logged at `[-5, -42]`, near-equatorial, at T-400 — and false
of the water today, after 400 Myr of drift. The name is a fossil. Doc 01 has been
corrected to say so; the name itself is kept, because it is the rift's name and
the rift is what makes the front a *reunion* rather than an introduction.

### 3.2 There is no wall — there is a saddle at 1.3 km

The obvious reading of the map is that M1's cordillera stands between the cradle
(85–110 °W) and the throat (148 °W), and that reaching the coast means crossing
7.66 km of mountain. **It does not.**

Flood-filling Meridian land westward from the cradle under a height ceiling:

| Ceiling | Throat reachable? | Land reached |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1.0 km | **no** | 15,052 cells |
| **1.3 km** | **yes** | — |
| 2.0 km | yes | 37,421 cells |
| no limit | yes | 43,837 cells |

Bisecting the ceiling puts the pass at **1.31 km**. The connected low route from
the cradle to the western seaboard tops out there — below the 2 km line that
defines M1 at all, so the route never enters the cordillera. (Ground under
1,000 m exists at every longitude in the 20–34 °N band, but it is not linked
continuously below that height; the saddle is the join.)

**And The Deep sits at 2,090 m.** The homeland's reliable refuge stands **790 m
higher than the hardest ground on the only road out.** They were not merely
capable of the passage — they were over-equipped for it, by a lineage-defining
margin, before they ever needed it. Doc 03 §5 calls altitude tolerance
"ancestral, not acquired," and doc 04 §2 grounds that in The Deep. This is what
it was for.

The road is **5,225 km** from mid-cradle to the throat.

### 3.3 The crossing is 75 km, not 213

The strait holds islands — 148 island cells within the throat's frame. Solving
for the route that **minimises the longest single open-water leg**:

> **213 km** shore to shore direct · **75 km** as the longest leg once the
> strait's islands are used as stepping stones.

Seventy-five kilometres is a real maritime problem and a solvable one — squarely
in the band Earth's first ocean crossings occupied. Doc 01 §5 names the hotspot
chains as "sweepstakes routes" and calls this front the planet's "weakest
filter"; that is now quantified. It is not a leap. It is a chain, and the
technology it demands is *repeatable short-water craft*, not deep-ocean
seafaring — which is exactly the distinction doc 03 needs when it makes Sirocca
and Borea qualitatively harder later.

### 3.4 They leave from a desert

The throat shore, 25–30 °N and 152–144 °W, in Köppen:

`BWh 48 % · BSh 15 % · BWk 14 % · Csa 6 % · EF 6 % · ET 5 % · BSk 5 %`

— **62 % true desert, 82 % arid.** The 11 % `EF`/`ET` is not coast: the box is
8° of longitude wide and catches the cordillera's western flank at its eastern
edge. Read it as the wall standing behind the beach, which is what it is.

On the far side is **V1a**, Selvana's Tropical North, **NPP 1578** — the most
productive province on the planet. The crossing runs from the driest coast on
Meridia to the richest ground in the world, across 75 km of island water.

## 4. The expulsion and the fan — T-200 to T-90 kyr

**INTERPRETED**, from §3's geography and doc 08's trigger.

The Sump's salinity crosses from hostile to unusable. The richer of the two
refuges is gone; what remains is safe and poor. They leave — and doc 08 is
emphatic that this is an **expulsion, not an achievement**.

They then have 110 kyr and 28.34 Mkm², and the continent offers two directions
that are nothing alike.

**East and downhill — the abundant direction.** Across the M3/M4 ecotone, which
doc 04 measures at **49.6 % mixed bins**: the softest major boundary on the
planet, and one the Aulacines had already worked from both sides. NPP goes
**537 → 1,353**. The coast goes 2.6 % → 9.1 %. This is where the people go,
because this is where the food is, and within a few thousand cycles it holds
most of them.

**West and level — the exit direction.** Along the 20–34 °N band, through the
1.3 km saddle, 5,225 km to a hot-desert shore looking at islands. There is
nothing to recommend this direction. It is drier than home, poorer than home,
and it ends at the sea.

**North — the frontier.** M2 offers **NPP 1,068** and a 95 % growing season, but
only **54 % frost-free** against a body built at 98 %. It is reachable,
productive and permanently marginal: a seasonal frontier worked from the south,
not a second homeland.

**And M1 is not a home at all** — NPP 331, 21 % frost-free, 100 % above 2 km,
**0.0 % coastal**. It is passed through, at its edges, or not at all.

### The asymmetry, which is the point

> The direction the people go and the direction the species goes are **opposite**.

Meridia's demographic centre of gravity moves **east**, into M4's lowlands, and
stays there. But the thread that eventually reaches Selvana — and through
Selvana, eventually, the world — runs **west**, down a 5,225 km corridor into
the most marginal country on the continent, and gets on the water.

**The peoples who cross are not from the core.** They are from the arid western
fringe: a small, dry, peripheral population, unremarkable at home, that happened
to be standing at the one place on 28 million km² of coastline where the next
continent is 75 km away in short hops. And they could get there at all because
of a tolerance for salt and height they inherited from a lake basin their
ancestors had left half a million years earlier.

## 5. What this hands the next layers

- **Mobility is the baseline, sedentism is the innovation.** The founding habit
  is a long vertical annual circuit. Any Meridian society that stays put is doing
  something new.
- **The domestication relationship predates the dispersal by 600 kyr.**
  Flushgrass and the Rainherd are not discoveries. The culture layer inherits a
  species that has known its future crops and herds for six thousand cycles.
- **Two Meridian population centres, unequal.** A large eastern one in M4 and a
  small western one at the throat, connected by 5,000 km of arid corridor. That
  is a plausible root for the deep regional structure doc 08 §5 requires — and
  the *founder* population of every other continent comes from the small one.
- **The exit technology is short-water craft, not seafaring.** 75 km in hops.
  Reserve real blue-water capability for the Eastern Ocean at ~30 kyr, where doc
  03 §7 needs it to be the world's hardest and most consequential act.
- **A myth-shaped fact, free of charge.** The last of Meridia is desert; the
  first of Selvana is the richest land on the planet. Whatever the peoples
  remember about the crossing, the contrast is real and it is measured.

## 6. What this fixes, and what comes next

**Fixed (the Meridian interval):**

- **710 kyr — 89 % of the species' existence — on Meridia**, of which **600 kyr
  (75 %) on 2.24 Mkm²**: 8 % of one continent, ~2 % of the planet's land.
- The basin phase is a **two-refuge annual circuit** across 1,362 km and 1,843 m,
  making **long vertical transhumance ancestral** rather than invented, beside a
  volcanic field that never stops producing stone and a salt sea that is quietly
  dying.
- **The throat is at 27.5 °N, 148.4 °W** — 213 km direct, and **75 km as the
  longest leg** once the strait's islands are used. The Western Sea is *widest*
  at the equator; doc 01's "Equatorial" is the rift's name, not the water's, and
  has been annotated.
- **There is no wall.** The connected low route from cradle to coast tops out at
  **~1.3 km**, never entering M1 — and **The Deep, at 2,090 m, is 790 m higher**.
  The homeland over-equipped them for their own exit.
- The corridor is **5,225 km** and ends on a shore that is **62 % true desert**,
  facing **V1a at NPP 1,578**.
- **The core goes east; the founders come from the west.** The population that
  peoples three continents is a marginal fringe of the one that stayed.

**Open:**

- **The behavioural package is still unwritten** — fire, language, and what
  600 kyr of one circuit does to a mind. Doc 08 flagged this; this document has
  now spent the interval without filling it.
- **The eastern and western Meridian populations need names and a relationship.**
  This document creates them and leaves them anonymous.
- **Selvana's side of the crossing is unwritten.** V1a is the richest province on
  the planet and has no ecology (`docs/life/` has M3, V3, S2, B1). Landing there
  from a desert is the culture layer's first contact scene and the life layer
  cannot yet describe the ground.
- **B2 and M4** remain the standing ecology debts from docs 07 and 06.
